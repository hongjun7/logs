# Light theme and bilingual editorial revision

The site keeps its existing routes and begins in a light theme regardless of
the operating system setting. A manually selected dark theme is remembered
across languages. The front page presents a reading list: warm paper, dark ink,
the existing green accent, and thin rules between entries. The article’s
equations, examples, and figures provide its visual detail. The design pass
followed [unslop by Damir Sokolovsky](https://github.com/heydamir/unslop)
([CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)).

All 13 article pairs have noun-phrase subtitles and a new worked example
or a step-by-step explanation at the relevant section. The Korean prose uses
formal polite endings. The narrative tracks the original claims and keeps
original benchmark conditions, publication dates, and source-review dates.
New references are linked beside the explanations they support. A new math
example is not a replication of a reported experiment.

Local verification: Jekyll build, repository site and bilingual checks,
math/code/source parity checks, numerical checks, and JavaScript syntax check.
All 26 article routes and 430 existing element IDs were preserved, as were
the original equations, fenced code, and cited URLs. Each of two browser
viewports (1200 and 390 CSS pixels) rendered all 26 editions and 1,786 math
expressions without MathJax errors or document-wide overflow. The homepage,
archive, search, and tags were also checked at 320 pixels. Search stayed
within the selected language. A dark OS setting did not change the default;
an explicit dark setting persisted after switching languages.
